A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.

How do I place an order?+

Add gadgets to your bag, enter your details, and continue to WhatsApp. Review and send the prepared message. Your seller will confirm availability and arrange payment.

Can I inspect a used phone before buying?+

Ask your seller for actual device photos, battery health, repair history and inspection options on WhatsApp. Arrange an inspection or pickup where available before committing.

How do delivery and payment work?+

Delivery fees, timelines, payment methods and pickup availability are confirmed directly with the seller. The bag total covers products only; no payment is collected on this site.

Do products come with a warranty?+

Warranty and return terms vary by device and seller. Check the product details and ask the seller to confirm the applicable terms before payment.
